# Willowbank Community Garden: plot holder's handbook

A made-up garden, written for Nasute's demo questions. Every fact here is invented, so a system can only answer from this page, not from what a model already knows.

## Opening hours

The garden is open every day from 7 am until dusk. The gate is locked overnight. Plot holders may come in earlier during a heatwave, after asking the coordinator.

## Fees

A full plot costs $40 a year and a half plot costs $25. Fees are due by 1 March. A plot holder who has not paid by 1 April loses the plot, which is offered to the waiting list.

## Water

The garden has six taps. From December to February, hoses may be used only before 9 am and after 5 pm; watering cans may be used at any time. Sprinklers are never allowed.

## Compost

There are three compost bays. Bay 1 takes fresh green waste, bay 2 is being turned, and bay 3 is ready to use. Meat, dairy and citrus never go in the compost.

## Working bees

A working bee is held on the first Saturday of each month, from 9 am to noon. Every plot holder comes to at least four a year.

## Tools

The tool shed key is kept by the coordinator. Tools go back in the shed by dusk on the day they are borrowed.

## Pests

Synthetic pesticides are not allowed anywhere in the garden. Neem oil and soap sprays are allowed.

## Unused plots

A plot that is not worked for 8 weeks gets a reminder. After 12 weeks it is offered to the next person on the waiting list.

## Bees

A beehive stands near the north fence. Keep at least 3 metres away from it.

## The coordinator

The coordinator runs the garden day to day and is elected each year at the annual meeting in June.
